# Games & Simulation Developer

This work was completed by Harish Chaurasia (Founder) in his individual capacity — prior to Arka Forge — for Los Alamos National Laboratory, in partnership with Arizona State University&apos;s School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks.

## Role

Games and simulation developer, building interactive environments and simulation infrastructure for complex systems. Simulation engineering and game technology applied to manufacturing workflows, training environments, and system digitization.

## Context

Los Alamos National Laboratory operates at the forefront of scientific computing and research. The collaboration with ASU&apos;s School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks brought simulation and gamification to workforce training and process optimization in advanced manufacturing.
