# Games & Simulation Developer

This work was completed by Harish Chaurasia (Founder) in his individual capacity — prior to founding Arka Forge — for Los Alamos National Laboratory, in partnership with Arizona State University&apos;s School of Manufacturing Systems and Networks.

## Role

Games and simulation developer, building interactive environments and simulation infrastructure for complex systems. Simulation engineering and game technology applied to manufacturing workflows, training environments, and system digitization.

## Impact

This project demonstrated the potential of digital twins and gamified simulation for real-world training and optimization challenges in advanced manufacturing. It informed the technical foundation and vision for Arka Forge.
